04Development Process

A process with
no surprises.

Five phases, each with a defined output. You always know where the project stands and what happens next.

  1. Phase 01

    Discover

    Two weeks of deep immersion — users, constraints, data, risk. Ends with a technical architecture and a fixed-scope plan you can hold us to.

    → Architecture & delivery plan

  2. Phase 02

    Architect

    System design, data models, and interface foundations locked before a line of feature code is written. Decisions documented, trade-offs explicit.

    → Design system & technical spec

  3. Phase 03

    Build

    Focused build cycles with a working product from week one. Weekly demos, open repositories, and no black boxes — you watch it come alive.

    → Working software, every week

  4. Phase 04

    Harden

    Load testing, security review, observability, and failure drills. We break it before your users can, then launch with confidence.

    → Production launch

  5. Phase 05

    Evolve

    Post-launch, we stay: measuring, optimizing, and shipping the next capability. Software is never finished — only improving.

    → Continuous improvement

08FAQ

Answers, before
you ask.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly — we reply within one business day.

What kind of companies do you work with?

We partner with funded startups, scale-ups, and enterprise teams building software where quality is non-negotiable — fintech, healthcare, logistics, developer tools, and AI-native products. We take on a small number of engagements at a time so every project gets senior attention.

How do engagements typically work?

Most engagements begin with a two-week Discovery sprint that produces a technical architecture, delivery plan, and fixed-scope proposal. From there we work in focused build cycles with a dedicated senior team, weekly demos, and full transparency into progress, code, and decisions.

Do you build production AI systems or just prototypes?

Production systems. We design for evaluation, observability, guardrails, and cost from day one — retrieval pipelines, fine-tuning, agent orchestration, and the infrastructure to run them reliably at scale.

Who will actually work on our project?

Senior engineers and designers only — the people you meet in the first call are the people who ship your product. We do not hand projects to junior teams or outsource delivery.

Who owns the code and IP?

You do — completely. Every engagement transfers full ownership of source code, infrastructure, models, and documentation. We build in your repositories and your cloud accounts from day one.

What happens after launch?

Every launch includes a stabilization period, complete documentation, and a handover program for your team. Most clients continue with an Evolve retainer: dedicated senior capacity for iteration, optimization, and new capabilities.
